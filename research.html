Work in Progress

Acquisitions and Labor Outcomes: Evidence from Surviving Target Firms in Brazil

Using Brazil's ownership registry linked to matched employer-employee records, I study the impact of acquisitions where target firms survive as separate legal entities. Matched event-study estimates show significant post-acquisition gains in employment and average wages, and a strengthening of the management layer.

Impact of Trade Liberalization on Firms' Internal Organization in Brazil

Exploiting variation in import tariff cuts across industries in 1990s Brazil, I estimate a long-run difference-in-differences regression on firms' internal organization. I find no change in the number of hierarchical layers, but employment losses concentrate at the bottom layer.

Workplace Networks, Diverse Employment Histories, and Job Referrals

with James E. Rauch

We study whether diversity in coworkers' employment histories expands job referral opportunities for workers displaced by plant shutdowns. Specifically, we test whether displaced workers disproportionately find jobs in industries and municipalities where a former coworker previously worked.
