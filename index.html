Ruby Mittal

Hello! I am a PhD candidate in economics at UC San Diego. My research is in labor and development economics. My current work uses matched employer-employee data from Brazil to study how acquisitions and trade liberalization affect workers and firms' management layers, and the role of workplace networks in job referrals. I am also interested in how firms hire and promote, such as how they identify and select managers, and why job descriptions often differ from the tasks workers actually perform.

Before my PhD, I was an ODI Fellow at Ethiopia's Ministry of Education, where I helped evaluate and improve its national education program, GEQIP-E. Earlier, I was a Research Associate at J-PAL, working on two randomized controlled trials in Delhi and Haryana. I hold an MSc in Economics for Development from the University of Oxford and a BA (Honours) in Economics from the University of Delhi.

Outside of economics, I enjoy painting, traveling, and catching sunsets.
